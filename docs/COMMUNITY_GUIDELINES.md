# Community Reporting Guidelines

Raksha Sutra community reports help everyone make safer choices about where they go. To keep this feature useful and fair, every report is reviewed before it reaches the public feed.

## What you can report

- Poorly lit streets, alleys, or public areas.
- Isolated or deserted locations, especially after dark.
- Broken or missing infrastructure that affects safety, such as no CCTV, broken streetlights, or no security presence.
- General safety patterns you personally noticed in an area, for example: “very few people around after 9 PM”.
- A safety incident you witnessed or experienced, described by location and situation only, without naming any individual.

## What you cannot report

- A report naming or accusing a specific person.
- A negative report about a specific business or shop without verified evidence.
- Rumours, hearsay, or something you did not personally witness.
- A personal dispute, such as a conflict with a neighbour, ex-partner, or acquaintance.
- Hate speech, discriminatory remarks, or content targeting a community or group.
- Exaggerated or fabricated content intended to make an area seem more dangerous.

## Simple rule

**Report the place or situation—not the person.**

## Moderation and anonymous reports

Reports that break these guidelines are removed and are not published. Repeated misuse may lead to a warning or account suspension. Reports naming people can be removed regardless of intent to protect against defamation and misuse.

Anonymous reports receive exactly the same moderation standard as named reports. The author’s identity is never shared publicly, including with a person or place mentioned in the report.
